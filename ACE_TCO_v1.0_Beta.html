AppleCare for Enterprise - TCO Analysis

How to use this tool

This tool models the total cost of ownership difference between self-insuring against device incidents and purchasing AppleCare for Enterprise (ACE). The numbers are estimates based on public industry benchmarks. The goal is to surface costs that customers don't always track and frame the conversation around real value.

1
Build the fleet
Add a line for each device model in the deployment. Set quantity and desired ACE attributes (term, tier, service pool). One model per line. Add lines for additional lines of business and models. Pricing auto-populates from the current SKU list.
2
Set the environment
Pick a deployment preset (Office, Healthcare, Field, etc.) to load industry-standard defaults, or go straight to the assumption sliders below to customize breakage rates, costs, and lifecycle inputs manually.
3
Read the results
Results update live as you adjust inputs. Use the Executive Summary for the CFO conversation. Use the Mac and iOS tabs for deeper dives with IT directors.

TCO Analysis

Industry benchmarks: SquareTrade, Asurion, IDC, Gartner, Forrester, Robert Half, BLS, apple.com/support

ACE SKU pricing: US Apple List Price (ALP)

This analysis excludes compliance documentation labor, executive escalation handling, security incident risk from devices in transit, and cost of capital on the spare pool. These typically add 10-20% to total self-insurance cost. The savings shown are conservative.
